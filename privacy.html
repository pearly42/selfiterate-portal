Personal Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked to you ("Personal Information") in a variety of ways when you interact with our Services. The Personal Information we collect depends on how you use the Services and may include the following:

Information You Provide to Us

This may include the following:

Contact and profile information: your name, email address, and the profile details you choose to enter — date of birth, sex, height, body weight, injuries or limitations, training goals, experience level, a short bio and a profile photo. Sign-in is handled by Apple, Google, or an email address and password; we never see your Apple or Google password.

User-generated information: what you create in the app — workouts and programs you build, exercises you add, the sets you log, session history, body weight entries, body measurements, notes, and progress photos. Sponte has no social feed and no community features; nothing you log is published anywhere. A workout card you choose to share is exported by you through your own device's share sheet.

Payment and transaction data: information about subscriptions you buy through the Services. We do not collect, process, or store payment card numbers. Payment is handled entirely by the platform you bought through — Apple (App Store and StoreKit) or Google (Google Play Billing) — each under its own privacy policy. Subscription state reaches us through Qonversion, which acts as an entitlement broker: it tells us which tier your account is entitled to, and never handles your card details.

Communications: Any messages, feedback, or inquiries you submit to us.

Device and usage information: when you visit our website, we or our hosting provider record information such as your IP address (which may be used to derive your approximate geographic location), device type, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, links clicked, and the dates and times of your visits. The mobile app does not report screens viewed, taps, sessions, or crashes to us or to anyone else; the only device information it stores is a per-install identifier and a device name, used to enforce the one-device limit on the free and Basic tiers. Our service providers keep operational logs, including the IP address a request came from, for the time needed to deliver and secure the Services. We do not collect precise GPS-based location data.

Inferences: We may make inferences based on the information we collect.

Information Collected Automatically

When you access or use the Services, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

Website device and usage information, such as your IP address (which may be used to determine general location), browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, links clicked, and the date and time of your visits;

Cookie and similar technologies on our website, limited to the storage needed to remember your theme preference and to serve the pages you request. We do not use cookies, pixels, or tags for advertising or for cross-site measurement; and

No analytics or advertising SDKs in the app. The Sponte mobile app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no advertising network, and no cross-app tracking. It does not read your advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA or the Android Advertising ID) and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The only third parties the app communicates with are Google Firebase (authentication, and on the Premium tier cloud storage of your own training data), Qonversion (subscription entitlement), and the App Store or Google Play (sign-in and purchases). If this changes we will update this Privacy Policy before the change ships, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Information from Third-Party Integrations

With your permission, the Services connect to the health platform on your device — Apple Health (HealthKit) on iPhone, or Health Connect on Android — to exchange a narrow set of records (collectively, "Health Information"). With that permission granted, Sponte reads your body weight, so you do not have to log it twice, and writes the workouts you complete, the active calories they burned, and body weight you entered in Sponte, so that your training counts toward your daily activity. Nothing else is read or written. The connection is off until you turn it on in Settings, and you can revoke it at any time in the Health app on iOS or in Health Connect on Android. Health Information held by those platforms is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

How we handle Health Information. We use it only to show you your own training and body-weight history inside Sponte. We do not use Health Information for advertising or marketing, we do not use it for data mining, we do not sell it, and we do not disclose it to third parties other than service providers storing it on our behalf as described under "Where Your Information Is Stored", or where you direct us to. Health Information is never stored in iCloud.

Where Your Information Is Stored

Free and Basic: on the free tier and on Basic, your training data — workouts, programs, exercises, session history, body weight, measurements and progress photos — is stored and processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers in the ordinary course of providing the Services. Your account record (email address, subscription tier, and the device holding the account) is still held by us, because an account is the thing you sign in to.

Premium: if you subscribe to Premium, that training data is also stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), acting as our processor, so that it follows your account across devices and reinstalls.

Health Information: body weight read from your device's health platform is kept alongside the rest of your training data, which means it stays on the device on Free and Basic, and is stored with our processor on Premium. The workouts and calories Sponte writes into the health platform live in that platform's own store, under its rules, not ours.
